Shower Glass Descaling

Bring cloudy, calcium-etched shower doors back to 100% crystal-clear transparency.

Hard Water & Mineral Removal

Hard water minerals and soap scum chemically bond with glass pores over time, creating a frosted, stained appearance that regular glass cleaners cannot remove.

Our restorative process buffs away bonded calcium deposits without scratching the glass, followed by a hydrophobic nano-coating that repels water droplets and stops future staining in its tracks.

  • Complete calcium & lime scale removal
  • Ultra-smooth hydrophobic protective coat
  • Anti-mildew sanitary perimeter silicone re-caulking
