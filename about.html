Our Evolution & Technology Milestones

When We Started & How We Upgraded

From a humble single-van operation to an industry-leading restoration fleet equipped with commercial truck-mounted thermal boilers and precision nanotech.

2014 • The Founding Vision

Started with a Single Van & a Non-Remodel Mission

PureTile was launched with one van and a foundational conviction: tile and grout shouldn't need costly replacement simply because of dirt and oxidation. We proved that deep enzymatic chemistry could revive surfaces that homeowners thought were beyond saving.

🌱 1 Van & 100% Focus on Non-Destructive Tile Restoration
2017 • Technology Upgrade

Upgraded to 220°F Truck-Mounted Hydro-Extraction

Retired traditional handheld manual scrubbers in favor of custom-engineered truck-mounted boiler units. Delivering continuous 220°F water at 1,200 PSI, this breakthrough instantly emulsified biological oils and deep-seated bacteria in seconds.

⚡ 1,200 PSI Pressure & 220°F Instant Thermal Sanitization
2020 • Material Innovation

Restoration Workflow

From Stained to Brand-New

A seamless, meticulous 4-step workflow ensuring guaranteed before and after results for every home and facility.

01

Surface Pre-Treatment

We apply targeted bio-enzymatic agents that emulsify bonded soap scum, hard water minerals, and grease.

02

Rotary Hydro-Scrub

High-temperature pressurized water jets dislodge deeply seated impurities from micro-pores in the grout.

03

Vacuum Extraction

Industrial vacuums immediately extract all dissolved grime and water, leaving surfaces spotless and fast-drying.

04

Protective Sealing

We apply penetrating sealers, hydrophobic glass coats, and mold-proof silicone to lock in freshness.
